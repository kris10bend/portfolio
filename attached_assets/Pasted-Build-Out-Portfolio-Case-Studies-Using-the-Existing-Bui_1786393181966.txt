PROJECT 1 — JOURNEY TO NET ZERO
Title
Journey to net zero
Tagline
Bringing the UAE’s vision for a net zero future to life at COP28.
Provocation
How might we connect the UAE’s deep-rooted culture of resourcefulness with its vision for a net zero future?
Scope
Creative Direction · Experience Design · Exhibition Design · Content Strategy
Role
Creative Director
Client
UAE Pavilion, COP28
Year
2023
Description
When our team joined the UAE’s Journey to Net Zero exhibition, the space was already in development—but its story wasn’t yet resonating with the leadership team. We quickly onboarded to the challenge of telling a national sustainability story that connected the UAE’s past, present, and future: from the resourcefulness and resilience of its ancestors to the innovators shaping its path toward net zero.
As Creative Director, I partnered with strategy to shape the narrative and carried it through to the final experience, translating the story into every layer of the exhibition. Working alongside interior architects and additional creative partners, I designed and creative directed the displays, films, archival storytelling, physical artifacts, innovator portraits, and data visualizations that brought the narrative to life across the gallery, connecting ancestral practices with the innovations shaping the UAE’s net zero future. We extended the experience across both the public-facing Green Zone and the delegate-only Blue Zone, adapting the story, content, and visual language for two very different audiences while maintaining a cohesive journey from heritage to a net zero future.

PROJECT 3 — EXPO 2020
Title
Expo 2020
Tagline
Translating breakthrough ideas into an exhibition celebrating the UAE’s innovators.
Provocation
How might we bring the UAE’s most ambitious innovations to life in ways people could understand, explore, and connect to?
Scope
Creative Direction · Exhibition Design · Experience Design · Content Strategy
Role
Creative Lead
Client
UAE Pavilion, Expo 2020 Dubai
Year
2021
Description
As Expo 2020 approached, our team joined the UAE Pavilion late in its development to help bring greater clarity and cohesion to the visitor experience. After the pavilion opened, an opportunity to create a new exhibition emerged: an Innovation Showcase highlighting the scientists, researchers, entrepreneurs, and makers shaping the UAE’s future. I stepped into the role of Creative Lead to develop the exhibition and translate their work into stories that could connect with a broad public audience.
Working directly with innovators across disciplines, I interviewed each participant to understand not only what they had created, but why it mattered. I then led the creative development of bilingual exhibition stories, custom illustrations, physical displays, and visual representations that made complex research and technologies approachable without losing their substance. Rather than presenting innovation as a collection of technical achievements, the showcase centered the people and ideas behind them, inviting visitors to discover work they might never otherwise encounter.

PROJECT 4 — COLLECTIVE INTELLIGENCE
Title
Collective Intelligence
Tagline
Transforming employee insights into collective action through human-AI collaboration.
Provocation
How might we use AI to create more space for people to think, make, and solve problems together?
Scope
Experience Strategy · Creative Direction · Experience Design · Visual Design · AI Experience Design
Role
Creative Director
Client
Confidential
Year
2026
Description
When an organizational survey surfaced recurring challenges around collaboration, communication, meeting culture, and strategic alignment, we saw an opportunity to turn those insights into a shared experience. Rather than presenting the findings as another report, we designed a process that brought people together to reflect, collaborate, and build solutions around the challenges they were experiencing.
As Creative Director, I led the creative development of the experience, designing its visual language, physical materials, and collaborative moments from gallery through pitch. Employees first encountered their own stories and perspectives through an immersive gallery, then worked together in facilitated sessions to identify challenges, explore possibilities, and develop ideas through an analog, highly collaborative process. A custom AI tool helped teams synthesize their thinking into a clear presentation, creating a bridge from brainstorming to consensus and pitch preparation. Each team then presented a 60-second pitch to the group, with participants voting on the ideas they believed could have the greatest impact.

PROJECT 5 — RADICAL IMAGINATION
Title
Radical Imagination
Tagline
Designing an interactive studio to build radical imagination in the age of AI.
Provocation
How might we help leaders challenge the assumptions that shape how work gets done—and imagine what could come next?
Scope
Experience Strategy · Creative Direction · Experience Design · Visual Design
Role
Creative Director
Client
SYPartners
Year
2026
Description
As AI rapidly reshapes how organizations work, we designed Radical Imagination Studio to help leaders practice a fundamentally different way of thinking—challenging inherited assumptions and imagining entirely new possibilities. The experience became part of the broader conversation around AI and leadership, hosted for our partners internally, as an intimate gathering for select clients, and as a public-facing event during New York’s AI Week.
Participants moved through a carefully sequenced studio experience—exploring an exhibition of emerging AI possibilities, completing an AI-powered leadership diagnostic, contributing to live visualizations of collective perspectives, and working in teams to reimagine how AI could reshape a fictional client challenge. Each activity shifted participants from observing change to actively experimenting with it, creating a space where leaders could experience Radical Imagination rather than simply learn about it.

PROJECT 6 — VIBE CAMP
Title
Vibe Camp
Tagline
Helping teams build together in the era of AI collaboration.
Provocation
How might we give teams the confidence and space to discover a new way of creating together with AI?
Scope
Experience Strategy · Creative Direction · Experience Design · Visual Design · AI Design
Role
Creative Director
Client
SYPartners
Year
2026
Description
As AI tools rapidly become part of creative work, we designed Vibe Camp to help teams move beyond individual experimentation and discover how they could create together in entirely new ways. Inspired by the spirit of summer camp, we transformed a day of technical training into an immersive environment designed to make experimentation feel playful, approachable, and collaborative. The environment itself was designed to lower the stakes, creating permission to experiment before participants ever touched the technology.
The day moved from guided learning to independent exploration and then collaborative creation. Participants learned the fundamentals of vibe coding in Replit by building their own birdwatching applications before working in teams on a design challenge focused on creating deeper human connection at a large client event. Using shared FigJam workspaces and Replit’s collaborative workflows, teams designed, built, and presented working prototypes together—experiencing firsthand how AI could become part of a new creative process rather than simply another tool to learn.
